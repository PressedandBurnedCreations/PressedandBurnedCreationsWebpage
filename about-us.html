2019 The Beginning of Our Journey

Our Story

Our story began in 2019 when we purchased a direct-to-garment printer to make T-shirts for our motorcycle riding club. What started as shirts for our members soon grew into making them for other customers—and sparked the idea for something bigger.

With Miles's background in drawing and about twenty years of experience in designing and tattooing, we continued finding new ways to bring artwork to life. We added a sticker printer, followed by sublimation equipment and laser engravers.

Along the way, we attended apparel and printing shows, learned new software, and developed our skills one project at a time.

As our business grows, we're proud to share this journey with our daughter and grandchildren. Building something together as a family makes this next chapter especially meaningful to us.

We're starting with bold, custom stickers, with more printed, pressed, and engraved products to come. From our family to yours, we look forward to helping you bring your ideas to life.

— Miles & Patty
